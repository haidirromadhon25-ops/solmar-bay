SOLMAR BAY

AN OPEN-WORLD CRIME STORY

Danny “Dash” Cole is back in Solmar Bay — broke, and the whole city owes somebody. Five jobs stand between him and owning these streets. Steal cars, run packages, outrun the Solmar PD, and take the Big Score. The city is yours to drive.

Progress saves automatically after each mission. Sound on for the full ride.

HOW TO PLAY

W A S D — walk / drive  ·  Mouse — look (click to lock, drag also works)
E — steal / enter the nearest car, exit the car  ·  Shift — run
Space — jump on foot, handbrake in a car  ·  1 / 2 — fists / pistol
Click — fire the pistol  ·  R — reload  ·  Esc / P — pause  ·  M — mute
Gold coins are cash pickups. The gold pillar marks your objective — follow the minimap.
Crimes raise your wanted stars: the Solmar PD sends cruisers, then officers on foot.
Stay clean for a while and the heat fades. Get busted or wasted and you pay a fine.
