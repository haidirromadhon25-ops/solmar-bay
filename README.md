Solmar Bay is an original open-world crime game built for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty. Explore a 720×720 coastal city with 9 avenues, beach and ocean — on foot or behind the wheel of stealable cars with drift and handbrake. Traffic AI and 22 pedestrians fill the streets, a 0–5 star wanted system sends police cruisers chasing and ramming, and officers can arrest you. Play 5 story missions ($300 → $2,500), fight the rival Sharks gang with pistol combat, grab cash pickups, and track it all on a live HUD with minimap, speed, ammo and wanted stars. WebAudio synth sound effects and a music loop, plus localStorage saves with Continue.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then open http://localhost:8000 in your browser.
